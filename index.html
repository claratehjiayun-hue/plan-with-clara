About关于我

Clara Teh

I trained in law at the LSE and spent years in corporate legal work, including estate matters. I saw how often families struggle not because there wasn't enough money, but because nothing was planned or written down. Now I help individuals and families put protection, savings and legacy plans in place - in plain language, at a pace that suits you. 我在伦敦政经学院（LSE）读法律，之后多年从事企业法务，也处理过遗产事务。我看到很多家庭陷入困境，不是因为钱不够，而是因为没有提前规划、没有写下来。现在，我帮助个人和家庭把保障、储蓄和传承安排好——用简单的话讲清楚，按你的节奏来。

FAQ

Common questions常见问题

Is the preview really free?初谈真的免费吗？

Yes. 10 minutes, no obligation. If a planning session isn't useful for you, I'll say so.是的。10 分钟，没有任何义务。如果规划会谈对你没帮助，我会直接告诉你。

What should I prepare?需要准备什么？

For planning: a rough idea of your monthly income and spending, your savings and EPF, and any policies you already hold. Approximate figures are fine.规划会谈：大概的每月收入和开销、储蓄与公积金，以及现有的保单。大约的数字就可以。

Online or in person?线上还是见面？

Online by default, on Zoom. In-person can be arranged after the preview.默认线上，使用 Zoom。初谈之后可以安排见面。

Which language?用什么语言？

English or Mandarin, whichever you prefer.英文或华语，看你方便。
